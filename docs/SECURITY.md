# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

| Version | Supported          |
| ------- | ------------------ |
| 3.x     | :white_check_mark: |
| < 3.0   | :x:                |

## Reporting a Vulnerability

BQ SQWAD TV and Bq Sqwad Technologies take the security and privacy of our viewers, channel producers, and content providers seriously.

If you discover a security vulnerability or privacy concern, please disclose it to us responsibly:

1. **Email**: Send your findings to **tv@bqsqwad.com**
2. **Subject**: `[SECURITY VULNERABILITY] <Brief Description>`
3. **Details**:
   - Step-by-step reproduction instructions
   - Affected endpoints, parameters, or mobile screens
   - Potential impact and severity
   - Any proof of concept (please do not exploit or access user data beyond what is strictly necessary)

### Our Commitment (SLA)
- We will acknowledge receipt of your report within **48 hours**.
- We will provide regular status updates as we investigate and remediate the issue.
- We will provide coordinated vulnerability disclosure once a fix has been verified and deployed.

### Safe Harbor
We consider security research conducted under this policy to be authorized. We will not pursue legal action against researchers who:
- Act in good faith to avoid privacy violations, data destruction, and service degradation.
- Do not access, modify, or retain customer data.
- Give us reasonable time to fix issues before public disclosure.
