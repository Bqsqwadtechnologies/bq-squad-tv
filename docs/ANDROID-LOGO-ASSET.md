# Official Android Logo Asset & Brand Pipeline

The approved BQ SQWAD TV brand assets are generated directly from `design/source/bq-sqwad-tv-logo.png` via `scripts/build-brand-assets.py` (and `scripts/build-brand-assets.mjs`).

## Assets Inventory
- **Android Mark (`drawable-nodpi/bq_sqwad_tv_mark.png`)**: Master brand mark with gradient backing and uncompromised aspect ratio.
- **Splash Drawable (`drawable-nodpi/splash_logo.png`)**: High-resolution splash asset for branded system launch.
- **Adaptive Launcher Icons (`mipmap-{mdpi,hdpi,xhdpi,xxhdpi,xxxhdpi}/ic_launcher*.png`)**: Density-specific adaptive icons maintaining full circular and rounded mask safety zones.
- **Monochrome Status Icon (`drawable/ic_stat_bq_sqwad_tv.xml`)**: Clean vector silhouette for Android status bar notifications.
- **Web Favicon & PWA Icons (`public/brand/`)**: 16px, 32px, 48px, 180px, 192px, 512px, maskable, and favicon.ico.
- **Open Graph Image (`public/brand/og-image.png`)**: 1200x630 social share card.

Clear space is preserved around the TV swoosh and play emblem; no stretching, distortion, or color alteration is permitted.
