# EPG & Playout Scheduling

## Schedule Slots {#schedule-slots}
- Programme items must be published at least 24 hours in advance to appear in the Electronic Programme Guide (EPG).
- Consecutive programmes must be scheduled back-to-back with zero time conflicts.
- Real-time updates push automatically to viewers' programme guide screens.
