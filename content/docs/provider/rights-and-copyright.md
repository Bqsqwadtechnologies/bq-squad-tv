# Rights, Licences, and Copyright Compliance

Broadcasters on BQ SQWAD TV must hold full legal rights and distribution permissions for all broadcast material.

## Rights & Licences Documentation {#rights-and-licences}
During channel onboarding and quarterly reviews, partners must provide:
- Distribution agreement or licence reference identifier.
- Authorized broadcast territories (e.g. Nigeria, West Africa, Worldwide).
- Licence commencement and expiration dates.

## Takedown & Counter-Notice Procedure {#takedowns}
If a rights infringement notice is received regarding broadcast content:
1. The affected stream or asset is temporarily paused pending review.
2. The partner receives immediate notification detailing the claim and claimant.
3. Partners may submit counter-documentation within 5 business days to confirm validity.
4. Repeat infringers face permanent channel suspension and account termination.
