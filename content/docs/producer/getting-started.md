# Getting Started with the Partner Console

Welcome to the BQ SQWAD TV Partner Platform. This guide outlines onboarding requirements, account verification, channel registration, and operational workflows.

## Account Types & Verification {#account-types}
The platform supports two distinct account classifications:
1. **Verified Business**: Established media networks, production studios, and commercial broadcasters. Requires commercial registration documentation and authorized representative identification.
2. **Personal / Independent Creator**: Solo content creators and independent channel operators. Requires government-issued national ID and identity verification.

## First Channel Registration {#first-channel}
Upon signing in with your approved partner email:
1. Navigate to **Channels** in the sidebar.
2. Select **Register Channel** to launch the guided channel onboarding wizard.
3. Supply technical ingest URLs, artwork assets, rating metadata, and licence confirmation.
4. Run the automated stream test to ensure manifest compatibility.

## Going Live {#going-live}
After submission, your channel enters a 24 to 48-hour compliance and technical review. Upon approval, your channel goes live automatically according to your configured schedule.
