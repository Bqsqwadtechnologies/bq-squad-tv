# Channel Promotion & Sponsored Placements

Partners can enhance discoverability through transparently badged promotions.

## Trending Promotions {#trending-promotions}
- **Sponsored Placement**: Featured slots on the viewer home screen and trending carousel.
- **Sponsored Badge**: All paid promotions display a visible "Sponsored" tag to ensure transparency for viewers.
- **Scheduling**: Promotions can be scheduled for specified day and time windows.
