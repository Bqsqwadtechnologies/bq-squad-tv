# Earnings, Statements, and Settlement

This guide explains how broadcast watch sessions are measured, audited, and settled.

## Settlement Rules {#settlement-rules}
Earnings are calculated server-side from verified playback heartbeats and settled according to your agreement terms.
- **Audited Minutes**: Only verified, active viewing minutes are eligible for settlement.
- **Statements**: Monthly statements are generated on the 1st of each calendar month covering the previous billing period.
- **Dispute Resolution**: If you identify a discrepancy in recorded playback time, open a ticket through the **Disputes** tab within 14 calendar days of statement issue.
