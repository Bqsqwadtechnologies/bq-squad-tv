# Age Rating & Child Protection Standards

BQ SQWAD TV maintains strict safeguards to ensure children are protected from unsuitable content.

## Age Rating System {#age-rating}
Every piece of broadcast content and live channel must carry an accurate age classification:
- **General (G)**: Suitable for viewers of all ages.
- **Parental Guidance (PG)**: Mild themes, suitable for family viewing.
- **13+**: Moderate drama, mild profanity, or non-explicit violence.
- **16+**: Mature themes requiring parental awareness.
- **18+**: Strictly mature content.

## Adult Content Designation {#adult-flag}
Channels broadcasting mature or adult-rated material must explicitly set the **Adult Content** flag during registration:
- **Kids Mode Lockout**: The platform automatically restricts accounts registered to viewers under 18 to Kids Safe Mode. Adult channels and 18+ content are never displayed, indexed, or suggested to child profiles.
- **Advertising Restrictions**: No adult channels may run advertising targeting youth audiences.
- **Verification Gating**: Only fully verified partner accounts in good standing may register adult-flagged channels.
